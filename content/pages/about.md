Foundry is where I write up engineering and research projects: what was built, why it was built that way, and what turned out to be harder (or easier) than expected.

I'm Lachlan O'Neill. You can find my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/lachjames).

The site itself is a few hundred lines of TypeScript that turn a folder of Markdown files into static HTML, served from GitHub Pages. There's a [post about how it works](/posts/hello-foundry/). If you want to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/feed.xml).
